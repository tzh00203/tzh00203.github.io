About Me

I am a master's student at the Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on LLM-assisted security analysis, aiming to make vulnerability discovery more reliable, auditable, and scalable.

I am broadly interested in program analysis, software and firmware security, trustworthy cyber agents, and security evaluation of LLM-based systems. My current work explores how to combine static taint analysis, source/sink modeling, CodeQL-style program analysis, Ghidra-generated pseudocode, and LLM reasoning for vulnerability discovery.

I am currently looking for research collaborations and visiting research opportunities in LLM security, program analysis, and cyber agent security.
