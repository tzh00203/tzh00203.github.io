About Me

I am a second-year master's student at the Network and Information Security Lab (NISL), Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Jianwei Zhuge. My research focuses on LLM-assisted security analysis, aiming to enhance the reliability, transparency, and practical effectiveness of LLMs and autonomous cyber agent systems. I also worked on Internet-connected device discovery, IoT security, and vulnerability discovery.

Previously, I received my B.E. in Cyberspace Security from XUTELI School, Beijing Institute of Technology. I have research and internship experience in network security, red teaming, vulnerability mining, and AI security.

I am currently looking for research collaborations and visiting research opportunities in LLM security, program analysis, and cyber agent security.
